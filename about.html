ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making campus connections.

CampusFind is a student lost-and-found noticeboard. Students can post items they have lost or found, browse reports and contact the person who posted a report.

Hackathon prototype: This is a starter project. Before real college-wide use, add user login, admin moderation, privacy controls and secure contact handling.
